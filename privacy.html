Who we are

CreditKeys (creditkeys.online) is operated by Team Seven Marketing LLC. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Quiz responses. Answers to the pre-qualification questions (phone type, credit freeze status, SSN/ITIN status) so we can match you to relevant options.
  • Email address. Only if you choose to provide it after completing the quiz, to send your results and related follow-ups.
  • Usage data. Standard information like pages visited, referring site, browser type, and approximate location, collected automatically through cookies and similar technology.

How we use your information

  • To match you with credit-building options based on your quiz answers
  • To send you your results and relevant follow-up emails, if you opt in
  • To measure and improve how this site performs
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. When you click through to a partner (like Kikoff, CreditStrong, or Chime), that click is tracked so we can be compensated for the referral, standard practice for comparison sites, and any information you then provide is subject to that partner's own privacy policy, not this one. We may also share data with service providers who help us operate the site (such as email or analytics providers), under agreements that limit their use of it.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your quiz progress, measure traffic, and improve performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not work correctly without them.

Your choices

  • Every marketing email we send includes an unsubscribe link
  • You can request that we delete your information by contacting us at the address below
  • You can decline to provide your email at any point in the quiz, results are shown either way

Data retention

We retain quiz and email data for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described here, or until you request deletion.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
