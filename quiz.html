What CreditKeys is

CreditKeys is a free, independent comparison service for credit-building products. We are not a lender, a bank, or a credit repair organization. We do not extend credit, hold your money, or change anything on your credit report.

What we do is narrow down a short list. There are dozens of credit-building products and most of them won't accept or suit any given person. Our four questions rule out the ones that would reject you or that you can't use, so you're not applying blind.

How it works

  1. Answer four questions. Your phone number type, whether your credit file is frozen, whether you have an SSN or an ITIN, and your state. Every question is there because at least one provider turns people down based on that answer.
  2. We match you. We filter our partner list against your answers and show only what you're actually eligible for.
  3. You apply directly with the provider. Clicking a recommendation takes you to that company's own site. You apply with them, under their terms. CreditKeys is not part of that application.

There is no credit check anywhere on CreditKeys, and taking the quiz does not affect your credit score.

Who we compare

These are the providers currently in our comparison set. Terms, fees and eligibility are set by each company and can change — always confirm current details on the provider's own site before applying.

Kikoff

A revolving credit account built for establishing payment history, with no credit check to open. Kikoff reports account activity to the credit bureaus. Charged as a low monthly subscription.

CreditStrong

Credit builder accounts issued by Austin Capital Bank. Your monthly payments go into a locked savings account and are reported to the bureaus as installment loan payments; you receive the savings balance at the end of the term. No credit check to open, and ITIN is accepted in place of an SSN.

Chime Credit Builder

A secured Visa credit card with no annual fee, no interest and no minimum security deposit. No credit check to apply. It requires a Chime Checking Account with a qualifying direct deposit before the card can be used. Card activity is reported to the credit bureaus.

What we ask for, and what we don't

The quiz collects your four answers and, at the end, an email address so we can send your results. That's it.

We do not ask for or receive your Social Security Number, date of birth, bank details, income, or any payment information. Anything of that kind is collected by the provider you choose, on their own site, under their privacy policy — never by us.

How we handle what we do collect is set out in our Privacy Policy.

How CreditKeys makes money

CreditKeys is free to use. Our partners pay us a referral commission when someone we refer opens an account. That is how the site is funded, and it may influence which products appear and the order they're shown in. It does not change your eligibility results and it never costs you anything extra.
